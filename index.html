La Ganga Clips

A personal clipping tool for the @lagangaclips TikTok account. It finds the best moments of Kick live streams, cuts them into vertical clips and sends them to the account owner's TikTok drafts for review.

About Terms of Service Privacy Policy

How it works

  1. DetectWatches public Kick chat for sudden spikes in activity.
  2. CutBuilds a short vertical clip (9:16) around the moment.
  3. RateScores the clip; only clips scoring 8/10 or higher go on.
  4. DraftUploads the clip to the owner's TikTok drafts (inbox).
  5. ReviewThe owner edits, adds sound and publishes from the TikTok app.

La Ganga Clips never publishes anything on its own. It uses TikTok's official Content Posting API in upload mode, so every video waits in the account owner's TikTok inbox until they decide to post it or delete it.

The tool runs on a single computer owned by the account owner. It is not offered to the public and it does not access any TikTok account other than the one that authorized it.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

What we get from TikTok

When you connect your account through TikTok Login Kit, the app requests only these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, used to know which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send videos to your TikTok drafts.

TikTok returns an access token and a refresh token. The app does not read your videos, followers, messages, comments or any other account data.

Where it is stored

The tokens and your open ID are stored in a single file on the owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any server run by us, not sold and not shared with anyone. They are used only to call TikTok's API on your behalf.

Other services the tool uses

  • Kick: public chat messages and public stream video are read to find and cut clips.
  • Anthropic (Claude): chat text, a transcript and a few frames from the public stream are sent to rate each clip. No TikTok account data is sent.
  • Telegram: the finished clip and a short summary are sent to the owner's private Telegram chat.

How long we keep data

Clips are deleted from the computer automatically after 7 days and saved chat logs after 14 days. The TikTok tokens are kept until you disconnect the app or until they expire (TikTok refresh tokens last up to 180 days).

Your choices

  • Revoke access in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Ask for your stored tokens to be deleted by sending a direct message to @lagangaclips on TikTok.

Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18 and does not knowingly process data from minors.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Questions: direct message to @lagangaclips on TikTok.
